MultiParity icon ideas, round 1

20 ideas from Kitlig (Recraft V4.1 Vector). Each one large, then at 16, 24, 32 and 48 px on a light and a dark taskbar. Pick by number; the next round refines the picks.

1 parity squares

Two overlapping squares offset diagonally, one bright blue and one cyan, their overlap white, on a deep navy tile.

2 equals offset

A bold equals sign made of two thick rounded bars, the lower bar shifted slightly right, cyan bars on a deep navy tile.

3 claim lock

A simple document sheet with a folded corner, a small solid padlock overlapping its lower right, white sheet and bright blue padlock on a deep navy tile.

4 publish layers

Three stacked flat diamond layers seen in isometric view, the top layer bright blue and lifted with a short arrow pointing up, white lower layers, on a deep navy tile.

5 mp monogram

A geometric monogram of the letters M and P joined into one continuous thick shape with cut 45 degree corners like a heads-up display, cyan on a deep navy tile.

6 chunk hex

A tight cluster of seven hexagons, the center hexagon cyan and the others bright blue, small even gaps, on a deep navy tile.

7 branch merge

A version branch diagram: two thick lines that split from one dot and rejoin into another dot, cyan lines and white dots, on a deep navy tile.

8 sync square

A rounded square outline with two thick curved arrows around it forming a circle, bright blue arrows and a white square, on a white tile with deep navy shapes.

9 version shield

A shield shape containing two stacked horizontal bars like layers, white shield with bright blue bars, on a deep navy tile.

10 asset cube

An isometric cube with one face cyan, one face bright blue and one face white, bold and flat, on a deep navy tile.

11 parallel streams

Two thick parallel lines that bend apart in the middle and come back together, like lanes of work in sync, cyan and bright blue lines on a deep navy tile.

12 pixel p

A chunky pixel art letter P built from square blocks, a few blocks cyan and most bright blue, on a deep navy tile.

13 history stack

Three square tiles stacked and offset like a deck of cards, the front tile cyan, the ones behind bright blue and navy outlined, on a white tile.

14 claim brackets

A solid square framed by thick corner brackets on all four corners, as if reserved and locked in place, cyan square and white brackets on a deep navy tile.

15 split diamond

A diamond split vertically into two halves with a small gap, left half bright blue and right half cyan, on a deep navy tile.

16 publish folder

A simple folder shape with a bold arrow pointing up out of it, white folder and cyan arrow, on a bright blue tile.

17 linked squares

Two rounded square outlines interlocked like chain links, one cyan and one white, thick strokes, on a deep navy tile.

18 rewind clock

A circular arrow running counterclockwise around a small solid square, like going back to an earlier version, cyan arrow and white square, on a deep navy tile.

19 claim flag

A document sheet with a small triangular flag planted on its top edge, white sheet and cyan flag, on a deep navy tile.

20 balance halves

Two half circles facing each other and offset vertically, forming a balanced pair, bright blue and cyan, on a deep navy tile.
